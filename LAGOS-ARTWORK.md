# Lagos artwork: visual research and drawing decisions

Research and visual comparison: 30 October 2026. The approved design reference remains unchanged. Before/after desktop and poster captures are retained under ignored `work/refinement-before-*` and `work/refinement-after-*` paths.

- Civic Towers facade supplier: https://alumetal.ch/civic-centre-towers/ — Victoria Island waterfront context and facade materials.
- Civic Centre structural/visual context: https://www.arbico.ng/media/ARBICO_-60TH_Book_.pdf — arched roof, structural fins and waterfront building form.

They are not shipped, used as poster backgrounds, copied as textures or inserted into exports. No photographic rights are being claimed. ## Original illustration

The drawing is an editorial montage of landmarks across Lagos, not a geographic panorama, survey drawing or photograph of a single viewpoint. Architectural features were interpreted in original Canvas paths and shaded WAD green/gold materials.

- **Lekki–Ikoyi Link Bridge:** paired open pylon tips, linked upper shaft, flared legs around an open lower span, fourteen stay cables on each side, a continuous deck/parapet, approach piers, road lights and reflected light. The previous triangular mast/fan was removed.
- **NECOM House / Marina:** stepped twin facade planes, horizontal window bands and a central cylindrical communications shaft with a round crown and antenna. This replaces an anonymous rectangular tower.
- **Civic Towers / Victoria Island:** two green glass planes, a projecting podium, stepped crown and a tapered lattice spire.
- **Civic Centre / Victoria Island:** inverted terraced massing, a raised steel arch, structural fins and a glazed frontage. The previous generic ellipse was removed.

These foreground landmarks have distinct geometry. Low-contrast, receding waterfront frontage supplies depth without being presented as additional named landmarks. Grain, glow and water reflections use fixed calculations, so the artwork is deterministic. The same illustration scales within each of the three exact-size poster artboards, while the portrait and nameplate retain format-specific geometry.

## QA limits

All exported formats are inspected at their actual pixel sizes, along with desktop/mobile browser captures. Small landmark linework is intentionally subtler in a phone preview and clearer in the 1080-pixel-wide PNG. This is stylized architectural artwork, not an engineering representation. Portraits retain the uploaded background; there is no background-removal model or external processing service.
